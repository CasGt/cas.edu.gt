About Us

Serving Since 1995

With the purpose of helping find solutions to the country's problems, CAS and Campus Sur were created to strengthen the Guatemalan educational field. CAS was accredited by the Ministry of Education in January 1995, providing flexibility in its procedures and programs to allow for the development of curriculum and evaluation as well as teacher training. CAS has been providing academic excellence for over 25 years to enhance education in Guatemala.

Our Mission

To educate students, guiding the development of their bilingual, academic, social and professional competencies and fostering growth in their values as citizens committed to society and the environment.

Our Vision

To be the leader of innovative education in the South Coast region of Guatemala, providing students with a quality academic experience that prepares them for higher education and global citizenship, committed to the development of their communities.

Definition of Learning

Learning at CAS is a transformative process in which knowledge is acquired, consciousness is awakened, and skills are obtained that empower students to improve and fully engage with their communities, their country, and the world through critical thinking, problem-solving, and collaboration.

OUR INITIATIVES

With the aim of preparing bilingual leaders with critical thinking skills and a commitment to the development of their community, we offer the best-quality experience to prepare them for higher education and global citizenship.

CHESS

CAS promotes chess within clubs for children from 4th grade onwards. It is also offered as an extracurricular activity for parents and teachers.

ROBOTICS

The ideal endeavor for learning through play, promoting creativity and developing mathematical logic, spatial organization, directionality, and creative thinking.

NEWSELA

A powerful learning tool with personalized instruction that provides exclusive reading resources.

KHAN ACADEMY

A virtual resource aimed at developing or enriching learning in subjects such as mathematics, chemistry, and programming. The student learns at their own pace, and it provides valuable information to the teacher about the progress of their students.

MORNING MEETING

A 20-minute block in the morning where students can gather with their guide teacher to discuss upcoming events and CAS values, and also engage in silent or group reading. It is a time to organize and prepare for a day of learning.

Our programs

ECP

We offer high-quality education for pre-kinder, kinder and grade 1. We prioritize patience, supervision, and support to aid children in adaptation and skill development. Our objectives include fostering independence, confidence, and social adaptability.

Fostering independence and confidence while preparing children for primary education.

Elementary

Elementary education covers grades one through five. The curriculum focuses on bilingual literacy in grades one and two, and basic reading and writing skills in both languages from grades three to five. Remedial programs are available from grade two for students in need.

Foster basic bilingual skills and offer remedial support as needed.

Middle-School

Middle School (grades 6-8) aims to ignite interest in learning and research. A counseling program supports students through adolescence, preparing them for High School with independence and academic proficiency in both languages. Students have 3 hours of daily English instruction and access to tutoring and sports programs.

Ignite interest in learning and prepare students for high school.

High-School

High School's mission is to cultivate continuous learners in an atmosphere of respect and trust. Students are motivated to achieve academic, social, and vocational goals. They participate in various sports, with the opportunity for international competition, fostering travel and cultural exchange in Central America.

Cultivate continuous learners and promote academic, social, and vocational goals.
