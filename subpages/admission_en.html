Procedure for applying for new admission

Contact/Information

To start the process, contact the school office to schedule an appointment and tour the facilities.

Admission Tests

Schedule an appointment with the CAS office for the student to take admission tests (Tests include: Spanish, Math, and English).

Pre-Kinder

To be admitted to Pre-Kinder 1, students must be at least 3 years old. Students entering Pre-Kinder and Kinder are admitted after an interview with the parents.

Grades 1 to 12

Admission tests must be scheduled by appointment.

Approved Admission

If the student passes the admission tests, the parents will complete the necessary paperwork and submit the registration form.

Enrollment Process

Step 1: Application

Fill out the enrollment application form available on the website.

Step 2: Interview

Schedule an interview with the school’s administrative staff.

Step 3: Evaluation

The student must undergo an academic assessment based on the grade they are applying for.

Step 4: Results

The results of the assessment will be sent via email.

Step 5: Final Enrollment

If the results are positive, complete the enrollment by submitting the required documents.

Enrollment Requirements

National Students

Complete the application form on the school’s website.
Submit a recommendation form from the previous institution. (The form is available at CAS).
Complete the Acceptance and Payment Agreement form available at the secretary's office.
Complete the Educational Services Adhesion Contract (DIACO).
Copy of the current year’s report card.
Certificates of previous studies issued by the previous school (Ministry of Education).
Proof of payment clearance issued by the previous institution.
Original and recent birth certificate.
Medical certification and vaccination schedule.

International Students

Complete the forms on the school’s website.
Complete the Acceptance and Payment Agreement form available at the secretary's office.
Original birth certificate.
Medical certification and vaccination schedule.
Copy of the report card.
Certificates of previous studies (report card) signed and stamped by:
1. State education district where the school is located (abroad).
2. Guatemalan embassy in the state where the student resided (abroad).
3. Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Guatemala.

Parents or Guardians of the Student

Employment certification with parents' income or a copy of the business license with an income letter from the accountant.
Copy of the last three bank statements.
Copy of the Personal Identification Document (DPI) of both parents.
Copy of the NIT card (Parent responsible for the account).
1 recommendation letter from CAS parents.
1 recommendation letter from someone outside of CAS.

Agreement Signing

Sign the acceptance of rules and procedures and payment agreement at the school’s secretary. Complete the Acceptance and Payment Agreement form available at the secretary's office.

Tuition Payment

Pay the enrollment fee and educational support at the cashier before September 30th.
